Salary & Overtime Table

Monthly CTC (SAR) and hourly OT rate per role. Used to compute daily Employee Cost and Employee Cost %.

Calculation Logic

  • Sales W/O Tax = Gross Sales − Void − Tax.
  • Net Sales = Sales W/O Tax − Complimentary Discount − Promotion Discount.
  • ADT = Transactions ÷ Opening Days.
  • AT = Sales W/O Tax ÷ Transactions.
  • Sales mix % = Category Sales ÷ Sales W/O Tax.
  • Water Qty V/S Transaction = Water Qty ÷ Transactions.
  • Modifier % = Modifier Sales ÷ Sales W/O Tax.
  • Upsize % = (Grande + Jumbo) ÷ (Tall + Grande + Jumbo).
  • DC App variance = App total − POS total.
  • Cash Deposited Variance = Cash Deposited − Cash Sales as per POS.
  • Google Review Qty = Google Positive Review Qty + Google Negative Review Qty.
  • DC App Sales % = (DC App POS + DC App App) ÷ Sales W/O Tax.
  • Employee Cost = Σ (headcount × monthly CTC ÷ 30) + Σ (OT hours × role rate).
  • COGS / Wastage / Employee % = amount ÷ Sales W/O Tax.
  • Inventory variance % = |variance| ÷ Sales W/O Tax.
  • Estimated Lost Sales = Breakdown Hours × Daily Sales Target ÷ Planned Hours.
